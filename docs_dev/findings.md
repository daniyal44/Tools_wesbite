## 2. Release Status Taxonomy (Launched vs Upcoming/Beta)
- `launched`: Fully released and accessible to the public/developers.
- `beta`: Active public/private beta or invite-only waitlist.
- `upcoming`: Formally announced, under active research/training, or scheduled for release (e.g. Open-Claw, Grok 3, GPT-5 Orion, Claude 3.7, Falcon 3, Gemini 2.0 Ultra, Sora General API).
