## FAQ

**What is the recommended keyword density for SEO?**

Most SEO experts recommend a primary keyword density between 1.0% and 2.5%. Anything above 3.5% may trigger search engine over-optimization flags.

**Does this tool ignore common words like "the" and "is"?**

Yes! The built-in stopword filter automatically strips common English conjunctions and prepositions to highlight your true topical keywords.
