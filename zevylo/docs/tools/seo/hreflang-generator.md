## FAQ

**What is the purpose of hreflang tags?**

Hreflang tags inform Google and other search engines which language or regional version of a webpage to serve users based on their physical location and language settings.

**What is the "x-default" hreflang tag?**

The x-default tag serves as the fallback page for international visitors whose language or country does not match any of your specific localized versions.
