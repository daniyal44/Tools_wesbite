## FAQ

**Will converting JPG to PNG improve quality?**

No. PNG is lossless, but it cannot restore detail already lost to JPEG compression. It simply prevents further quality loss.

**Does the PNG have a transparent background?**

JPG files have no transparency, so the resulting PNG keeps the original solid background.
