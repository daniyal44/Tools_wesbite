## FAQ

**Is my cropped image uploaded to a server?**

No! All cropping and image rendering occurs 100% locally on your computer inside an HTML5 Canvas element. Zero data is uploaded.

**Does this cropper maintain image quality?**

Yes! Zevylo crops directly from the original full-resolution image pixels to preserve maximum sharpness.
