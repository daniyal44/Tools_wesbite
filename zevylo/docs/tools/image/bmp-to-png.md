## FAQ

**Why convert BMP to PNG?**

BMP files are uncompressed and take up huge amounts of storage space. PNG uses lossless compression, producing identical image quality at a fraction of the file size.

**Is conversion fast?**

Yes! Because conversion runs in your browser via HTML5 Canvas, large BMPs convert in milliseconds without upload delays.

**Can I convert multiple BMP files?**

Yes, batch conversion is fully supported.
