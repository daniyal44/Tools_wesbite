## FAQ

**What is AVIF and why should I convert it to JPG?**

AVIF is a high-compression image format based on the AV1 video codec. While it produces small file sizes, many older devices and image editing apps cannot open AVIF files. Converting to JPG ensures 100% compatibility.

**Can I convert multiple AVIF files at once?**

Yes! Batch conversion is supported with single-click download of all converted JPGs.

**Is my data secure?**

Yes, your images never leave your computer. Conversions happen 100% locally in your web browser.
