## FAQ

**Does the icon keep transparency?**

Yes. Transparency from the WebP source is preserved in the PNG-based ICO entries.

**Which sizes should I include?**

16 and 32 px are essential for favicons; 48 and 256 px help on Windows and high-DPI screens.
