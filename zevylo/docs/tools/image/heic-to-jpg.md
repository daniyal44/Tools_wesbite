## FAQ

**Why does my iPhone take HEIC photos?**

Apple uses HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) by default because it saves storage space. However, Windows PCs and many websites cannot open HEIC files without conversion.

**Can I batch convert multiple HEIC photos?**

Yes! Select multiple photos to convert them all to high-quality JPGs at once.

**Is my personal photo private?**

Yes! The conversion happens strictly in your browser. No photos are sent to any remote server.
