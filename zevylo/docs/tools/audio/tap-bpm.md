## FAQ

**How do I use the Tap BPM tool?**

Tap the large button or press the spacebar on your keyboard in rhythm with the beat of the song. After 3-4 taps, the tool calculates your tempo with high precision.

**How many taps are required for an accurate BPM?**

Tapping 8 to 16 times provides the most accurate average BPM calculation as it evens out slight human timing variations.
