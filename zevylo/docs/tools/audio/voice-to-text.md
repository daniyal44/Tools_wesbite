## FAQ

**Which browsers support voice to text?**

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari have native support for speech recognition via the Web Speech API.

**Is my audio uploaded or saved?**

No. Transcription occurs directly through your browser speech recognition engine. Zevylo never stores or records your voice audio.
