## FAQ

**What frequency range can this generate?**

It can produce any audible frequency from 20 Hz (deep sub-bass) up to 20,000 Hz (ultrasonic treble limits of human hearing).

**What waveforms are supported?**

Sine (pure tone), Square (rich odd harmonics), Triangle (mellow harmonics), and Sawtooth (full harmonic spectrum).
