## FAQ

**How accurate is the seconds countdown?**

It calculates exact seconds difference from midnight of your birth date to the current real-time system clock, updating dynamically every second.

**Can I track wedding anniversaries as well?**

Yes! Toggle the Celebration Type to "Marriage / Wedding Anniversary" to calculate elapsed marriage milestones and upcoming anniversaries.
