## FAQ

**How do I find overlapping business hours?**

Drag the 24-hour timeline slider. Green highlighted cards indicate local working hours (9 AM - 5 PM) across all selected cities.

**Does it automatically handle Daylight Saving Time (DST)?**

Yes, calculations use standard browser IANA time zone databases that account for active daylight savings adjustments automatically.
