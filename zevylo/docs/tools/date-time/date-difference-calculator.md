## FAQ

**How are business days calculated?**

Business days count Monday through Friday between the two specified dates, excluding Saturdays and Sundays.

**Can I include or exclude the end date?**

Yes! Toggle the "Include End Date" checkbox to add or exclude the boundary day from the total count.
