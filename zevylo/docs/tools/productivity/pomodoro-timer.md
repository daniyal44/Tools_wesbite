## FAQ

**What is the Pomodoro Technique?**

A time-management method that breaks work into focused intervals (traditionally 25 minutes) separated by short breaks, with a longer break after four rounds — helping you stay focused and avoid burnout.

**Can I change the focus and break lengths?**

Yes. Open Settings to set your own focus length, short break, long break, and how many rounds to run before the long break.

**Will the timer keep accurate time in a background tab?**

Yes. It tracks a target end time rather than counting ticks, so it stays accurate even if the browser throttles background tabs.
