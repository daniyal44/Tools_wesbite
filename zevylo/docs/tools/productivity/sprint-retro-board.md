## What it does

Direct alternative to Reetro.io! Host agile sprint retrospectives without signing up or paying subscriptions. Features four distinct retrospective columns: "What Went Well" (Mad/Sad/Glad or Start/Stop/Continue), "What Needs Improvement", "Action Items & Owners", and "Team Kudos & Shoutouts". Supports card upvoting, filtering, local storage persistence, and one-click export to Markdown, CSV, or formatted JSON for Jira and Slack.

## FAQ

**Is a login or account required to use this retro board?**

No login required! Your retrospective cards and votes are preserved securely in your browser’s local storage so you can run quick standups without registration.

**Can I export the retrospective summary to Jira or Slack?**

Yes, you can copy formatted Markdown with one click or download a CSV / JSON file of all columns, cards, authors, and vote tallies.
