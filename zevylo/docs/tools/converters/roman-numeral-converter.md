## FAQ

**What range of numbers is supported?**

Standard Roman numerals cover 1 to 3999. Numbers outside this range are rejected because classical Roman numerals have no standard symbol for larger values without an overline (vinculum).

**How do I convert a Roman numeral to a number?**

Switch to "Roman → Number" mode and type the numeral (e.g. MMXXVI). The decoded value appears instantly, and invalid forms are flagged.

**What is 2026 in Roman numerals?**

2026 is MMXXVI (1000 + 1000 + 10 + 10 + 5 + 1).
