## FAQ

**What is JSON-LD FAQPage schema?**

FAQPage is a Schema.org structured data format recognized by Google, Bing, and AI search engines that signals Q&A content, allowing your webpage to appear with rich expandable dropdowns in search results.

**Where do I paste the generated schema code?**

Paste the generated <script type="application/ld+json"> block inside the <head> or <body> tags of your HTML, or into your Next.js page component.
