## FAQ

**How do I get the YouTube transcript?**

On any YouTube video, click the "..." menu below the video and select "Show transcript". Copy the text and paste it directly into this tool.

**Is my video data sent to any third-party server?**

No. All parsing and structured summary formatting is executed 100% locally in your web browser. No audio or text leaves your computer.
