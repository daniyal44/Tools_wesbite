## What it does

Protect confidential documents, contracts, drafts, and invoices by applying customizable watermark stamps (such as "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", "COPY", or custom text). Customize opacity, angle (45° diagonal or horizontal), font size, and color. Generates download-ready watermarked PDFs client-side without any server processing. Stirling-PDF and TinyWow alternative.

## FAQ

**What watermark text presets are provided?**

Popular presets include "CONFIDENTIAL", "DO NOT COPY", "DRAFT", "APPROVED", "SAMPLE", or you can type any custom text.

**Can I adjust the watermark angle and transparency?**

Yes! You can choose between diagonal (45°) and horizontal (0°) orientation, and adjust opacity from subtle (15%) to prominent (60%).
