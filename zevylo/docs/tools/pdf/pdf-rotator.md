## FAQ

**Are my PDF files uploaded to a remote server to be rotated?**

No! All PDF modifications happen completely inside your browser using client-side binary stream manipulation. Your files never leave your computer.

**Can I rotate scanned PDFs?**

Yes! Both digital and scanned PDF files can be rotated 90° clockwise, 180° upside down, or 270° counter-clockwise.
