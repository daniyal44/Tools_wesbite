## FAQ

**How do I trim white margins from a PDF?**

Upload your PDF document, select "Trim White Margins" or set custom margin values, and click "Export Cropped PDF".

**Are my confidential documents uploaded to a server?**

No. The entire PDF cropping process takes place locally within your web browser.

**Does cropping reduce PDF quality?**

No, cropping adjusts document crop boxes and viewBox boundaries without rasterizing vector text.
