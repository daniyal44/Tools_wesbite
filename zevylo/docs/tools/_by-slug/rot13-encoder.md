## FAQ

**What is ROT13?**

ROT13 ("rotate by 13 places") is a simple substitution cipher that replaces a letter with the 13th letter after it in the Latin alphabet. Because 13 is half of 26, applying ROT13 twice restores the original text.

**Can I use custom rotation shifts?**

Yes! While 13 is standard for ROT13, you can drag the slider from 1 to 25 to perform any classic Caesar shift.
