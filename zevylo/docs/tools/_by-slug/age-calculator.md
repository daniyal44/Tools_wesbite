## FAQ

**How is chronological age calculated precisely?**

Chronological age compares the birth date directly against today’s date, factoring in leap years, month day variations (28-31 days), and daylight savings.

**Does this calculator find my birth day of the week and zodiac?**

Yes! Zevylo automatically determines the exact day of the week you were born on (e.g. Tuesday) and your astrological zodiac sign.
