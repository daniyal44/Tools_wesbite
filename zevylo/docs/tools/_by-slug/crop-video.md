## FAQ

**How do I crop a video for TikTok or Instagram Reels?**

Upload your video, select the 9:16 aspect ratio preset, adjust the crop center, and click "Export Cropped Video".

**Is my video uploaded to a remote server?**

No. The video is cropped frame-by-frame directly in your browser using HTML5 Canvas.

**Will cropping remove my original video quality?**

The cropped area maintains its original pixel clarity and exports at high bitrate encoding.
