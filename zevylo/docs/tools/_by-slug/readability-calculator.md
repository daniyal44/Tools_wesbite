## FAQ

**What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score for web content?**

A score of 60 to 70 is ideal for general web audiences (plain English, easily understood by 8th graders). Higher scores (80-100) are very easy, while scores below 50 are dense academic text.

**How does Flesch-Kincaid determine grade levels?**

It balances sentence length (words per sentence) with word complexity (syllables per word). Longer sentences and multi-syllable vocabulary increase the grade level requirement.
