**Are my photos uploaded to a cloud server?**

No! All filter effects and Canvas pixel rendering happen 100% in your browser. Zero photos are sent to external servers.

**Can I export the image at its original resolution?**

Yes! Zevylo applies filters directly to the native pixel resolution of your image when exporting to PNG.
