## FAQ

**Why should I remove EXIF metadata before posting photos?**

Photos taken with smartphones often embed your exact GPS location, camera serial number, and timestamp. Stripping EXIF protects your home location and personal privacy.

**Does this degrade the visual image quality?**

No! The actual pixel data is preserved at maximum quality while stripping binary metadata headers.

**Is my photo uploaded to your server?**

Never. The scrubbing process executes 100% on your local device inside your browser.
