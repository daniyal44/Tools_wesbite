## What it does

Detect whether text was generated by ChatGPT, GPT-4, Claude, or written by a human. Calculates burstiness (sentence variance), statistical perplexity, repetitive transitional markers, and highlights individual sentences likely created by AI models.

## FAQ

**How does this detector calculate the AI score?**

It calculates two primary linguistic metrics: Perplexity (vocabulary predictability and repetitive structures) and Burstiness (sentence length variance). LLMs tend to write sentences with uniform length and predictable transitions.

**Is my text stored or checked against online databases?**

No. The analysis is calculated entirely in your browser using client-side statistical linguistics.
