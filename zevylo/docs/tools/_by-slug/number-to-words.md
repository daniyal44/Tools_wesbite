## What it does

Convert numbers into written English words. Supports whole numbers up to the quintillions, negative numbers, and decimals (spoken digit by digit after "point"). A currency mode writes amounts as dollars and cents — ideal for cheques, invoices, and legal documents. Everything is computed locally in your browser with no rounding surprises.

## FAQ

**How large a number can it spell out?**

Up to the quintillions (18+ digits). Decimals are read digit by digit after the word "point".

**How does currency mode work?**

It writes the whole part as dollars and the first two decimal places as cents — for example, 1234.50 becomes "One thousand two hundred thirty-four dollars and fifty cents".

**Does it handle negative numbers?**

Yes. A leading minus is read as "minus" before the spelled-out value.
