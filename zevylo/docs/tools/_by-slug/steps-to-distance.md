## FAQ

**How many miles is 10,000 steps?**

For an average adult with a stride length of 2.1 to 2.5 feet, 10,000 steps equals roughly 4.5 to 5.0 miles (7.2 to 8.0 kilometers).

**How are calories calculated from steps?**

Calories are calculated using standard Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET) values combined with your body weight and walking pace.
