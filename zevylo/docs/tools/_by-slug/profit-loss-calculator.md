## FAQ

**What is the break-even formula?**

Break-Even Units = Total Fixed Costs / (Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit). It shows how many items must be sold to cover all expenses.

**What is the difference between Gross Margin and Net Margin?**

Gross margin only accounts for direct cost of goods sold (COGS), while Net margin subtracts all overhead, payroll, marketing, and taxes.
