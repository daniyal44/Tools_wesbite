## FAQ

**Are my images uploaded to any server?**

No. Both images are loaded entirely into your local browser memory using HTML5 canvas/blob URLs. Nothing is transferred over the network.

**Which comparison modes are available?**

Three modes: Interactive Draggable Split Slider, Classic Side-by-Side View, and Difference Blend (highlights differing pixels in bright contrast).
