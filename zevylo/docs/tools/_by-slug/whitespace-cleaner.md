## FAQ

**How do I remove extra spaces between words?**

Enable "Collapse spaces" and every run of two or more spaces (or tabs) is reduced to a single space, while line breaks are preserved.

**Can it remove empty lines?**

Yes. "Collapse blank lines" reduces multiple consecutive blank lines to one, and "Remove all blank lines" deletes every empty line.

**Can it join wrapped text into one paragraph?**

Yes. Turn on "Join into one line" to replace all line breaks with single spaces — handy for un-wrapping copied PDF or email text.
