## FAQ

**How does Bionic Reading work?**

Bionic Reading bolds the first few letters of each word, allowing the brain to recognize words faster using contextual memory, significantly speeding up reading flow.

**Can I export the converted text into Word or Notion?**

Yes! Use "Copy Markdown" or "Copy HTML" to paste the bolded formatting directly into Notion, Obsidian, Word, or Google Docs.
