## FAQ

**How do you convert hourly wage to annual salary?**

Assuming a standard 40-hour work week and 52 weeks per year (2,080 working hours): Annual Salary = Hourly Wage × 40 × 52.

**Can I calculate take-home pay after taxes?**

Yes! You can enter an estimated tax withholding percentage (e.g., 20% - 30%) to estimate your net take-home paycheck for every period.
