## FAQ

**Which image formats can I export to?**

You can convert your PDF into PNG (lossless), JPG/JPEG (compressed small size), or WebP (modern web format).

**Are my PDF documents uploaded to a server?**

No. The rasterization and extraction occurs 100% locally on your machine via HTML5 Canvas. Your documents remain strictly private.
