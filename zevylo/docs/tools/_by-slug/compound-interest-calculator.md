## FAQ

**What is compound interest?**

Compound interest is interest earned on both the initial principal deposit and the accumulated interest from prior compounding periods.

**Can I include recurring monthly contributions?**

Yes, this calculator allows you to input monthly deposits to simulate regular dollar-cost averaging into your investments.
