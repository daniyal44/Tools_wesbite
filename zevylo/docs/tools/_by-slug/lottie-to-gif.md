## FAQ

**What is a Lottie file?**

A Lottie file is a JSON-based animation format commonly exported from Adobe After Effects via Bodymovin, used for mobile apps and web animations.

**How do I convert Lottie JSON to GIF?**

Upload your Lottie .json file, preview the animation frames, and click "Export Animated GIF".

**Is my proprietary animation kept confidential?**
