## FAQ

**Will anyone be able to open my PDF without the password?**

No. The document is encrypted according to standard PDF security specifications; PDF readers will prompt for the password before rendering.

**Is my password sent to your servers?**

Never. Encryption occurs 100% inside your web browser. Neither your file nor your password ever leave your device.
