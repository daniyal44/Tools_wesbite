## FAQ

**Are my audio recordings or songs uploaded to a server?**

No. All audio decoding, waveform rendering, and slicing are performed strictly inside your browser using the HTML5 Web Audio API.

**What audio formats are supported for trimming?**

Supports all browser-decodable audio formats including MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, and FLAC, exporting the trimmed portion as a lossless WAV file.
