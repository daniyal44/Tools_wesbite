## What it does

Repeat a word, sentence, or block of text as many times as you need. Choose how the copies are separated — new lines, spaces, commas, nothing, or your own custom separator — and optionally number each repeat. Useful for filling test data, creating repeated captions, spamming-safe placeholder content, or bulk list generation. Runs entirely in your browser with live character and word counts.

## FAQ

**How many times can I repeat text?**

Up to 100,000 repeats. The result is generated instantly in your browser, so very large counts with long text may use more memory.

**Can I choose how repeats are separated?**

Yes — pick new line, space, comma, none, or type your own custom separator such as a pipe or dashes.

**Is my text sent anywhere?**

No. All processing happens locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.
