## FAQ

**How do I create a slow-motion video?**

Upload your clip, select a speed multiplier under 1.0x (such as 0.5x or 0.25x), and click Export. The video will be re-encoded in smooth slow motion.

**Can I speed up long videos to make a timelapse?**

Yes! Select 2x, 3x, or up to 4x speed to condense long clips into fast-paced timelapses.

**Are my files kept private?**
