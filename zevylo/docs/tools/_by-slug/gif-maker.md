## FAQ

**How do I create a GIF from photos?**

Select 2 or more images, adjust the frame delay (e.g. 200ms per frame), and click "Generate Animated GIF". The GIF will be created right in your browser.

**Can I reorder or remove individual frames?**

Yes! You can preview all uploaded frames, delete individual frames, or change delay times before encoding.

**Are my images uploaded to a server?**

No. The GIF encoding happens 100% locally on your computer with our lightweight client-side GIF89a engine.
