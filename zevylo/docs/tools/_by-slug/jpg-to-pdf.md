## FAQ

**How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?**

Upload your JPG images, reorder them if needed, select your page size (A4, Letter, or Fit), and click "Generate PDF Document".

**Is my data secure and private?**

Yes! The entire PDF generation runs inside your browser memory. No images or documents are uploaded to our servers.

**Can I choose the page orientation?**

You can select Portrait, Landscape, or Auto (which rotates each page to match the image dimensions).
