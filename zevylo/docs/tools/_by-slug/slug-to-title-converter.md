## What it does

Turn hyphenated URL slugs, file names, snake_case identifiers, and camelCase strings into clean AP Title Case, Chicago Title Case, sentence case, and start case. Corrects articles, prepositions, and acronyms automatically.

## FAQ

**What casing styles are supported?**

It converts your input into AP Title Case (Associated Press), Chicago Title Case, Sentence case, UPPERCASE, and Capital Case, automatically handling minor words (a, an, the, and, but, or, for, in, on, at, to).

**Can I convert multiple slugs in bulk?**

Yes! Simply paste multiple slugs or file paths separated by new lines to convert all of them simultaneously in batch mode.
