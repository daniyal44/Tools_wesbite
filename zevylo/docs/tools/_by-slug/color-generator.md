## FAQ

**How do I generate a new color palette?**

Simply press the Spacebar on your keyboard or click the "Generate" button. Any swatches you have locked will remain fixed while the others change.

**Can I export the colors directly into my CSS project?**

Yes! Click "Copy CSS Vars" to copy formatted :root variables (--color-1, --color-2...) straight into your stylesheet.
