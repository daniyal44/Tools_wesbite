## FAQ

**How is monthly EMI calculated?**

Monthly EMI is calculated using the formula E = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the tenure in months.

**Does this calculator include an amortization schedule?**

Yes! Zevylo displays a complete year-by-year amortization breakdown showing opening balances, principal repaid, interest paid, and closing balances.

**Is my financial loan data stored on your servers?**

No. All calculations run 100% locally in your browser. Your numbers and personal financial calculations never leave your computer.
