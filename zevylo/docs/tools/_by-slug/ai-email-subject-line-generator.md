## FAQ

**Why do character counts matter in email subject lines?**

Mobile email clients (like Apple Mail and Gmail on iOS/Android) truncate subject lines beyond 40-50 characters. Keeping key hooks under 45 characters maximizes readability on mobile devices where over 60% of emails are opened.

**How are spam triggers evaluated?**

The generator checks for known spam filters triggers including excessive capitalization, multiple exclamation marks, and sensitive words (like "100% free", "guaranteed cash", "urgent risk").
