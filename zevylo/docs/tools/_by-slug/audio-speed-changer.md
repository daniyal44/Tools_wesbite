## What it does

Adjust playback speed and tempo of podcasts, lectures, and music tracks from 0.25x (slow motion) to 3.0x (fast forward). Features realtime preview, pitch lock toggle (preventing chipmunk distortion), and offline Web Audio rendering to download your modified track as a WAV file.

## FAQ

**Does changing audio speed change the voice pitch?**

By default, the pitch preservation toggle is enabled, keeping natural vocal tones without helium or deep-voice distortion.

**Can I download the modified speed file?**

Yes! Click "Download WAV" to export the rendered audio at your chosen playback speed directly to your device.
