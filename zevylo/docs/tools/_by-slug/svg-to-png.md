## FAQ

**How does high-resolution SVG scaling work?**

Because SVGs are mathematical vectors, scaling them up (2x, 4x, 8x) before rendering them to the HTML5 Canvas produces razor-sharp raster PNGs with zero pixelation.

**Can I keep transparent backgrounds?**

Yes! Zevylo preserves transparent alpha channels by default, or allows you to fill the background with white or custom colors.
