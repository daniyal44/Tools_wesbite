## FAQ

**What is the PEEL paragraph structure?**

PEEL stands for Point, Evidence, Explanation, and Link. It ensures each body paragraph in your assignment delivers a coherent, rigorously argued academic point.

**How does word count allocation work?**

Standard academic best practices reserve 10% for the introduction, 80% for body arguments, and 10% for the conclusion and implications.
