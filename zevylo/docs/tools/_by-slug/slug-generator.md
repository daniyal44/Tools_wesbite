## FAQ

**Why are clean URL slugs important for SEO?**

Clean, descriptive URL slugs (like /blog/how-to-bake-sourdough) improve click-through rates (CTR) in search results and provide clear relevance signals to Google and web spiders.

**Should I remove stopwords like "a", "the", and "of" from slugs?**

Removing unnecessary stopwords keeps URLs short and punchy, making them easier to read and share on social media without hurting keyword relevance.
