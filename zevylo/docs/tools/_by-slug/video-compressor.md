## FAQ

**Will my video be sent to a remote server?**

No! Unlike CloudConvert or FreeConvert, Zevylo re-encodes the video stream directly in your browser using local canvas and MediaRecorder hardware decoding.

**Which resolution should I choose for email or Discord?**

720p HD with balanced 1.2 Mbps bitrate offers the sweet spot between sharp visual clarity and compact file sizes under 25MB.
