## FAQ

**Can I play individual piano notes or full chords?**

Both! You can click or tap individual white and black piano keys, or select any root note and chord quality to play full polyphonic chords and see their notes highlighted.

**What chord types are included?**

Major, Minor, Dominant 7th, Major 7th, Minor 7th, Diminished, Augmented, Sus2, and Sus4 across all root notes (C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#, A, A#, B).
