## FAQ

**How do I download my resume as a PDF?**

Click "Print / Save as PDF". In your browser print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination, select Margins: None (or Default), and click Save.

**Is my personal data saved or stored online?**

No. Everything runs 100% client-side in your web browser. No names, phone numbers, or work experience are ever sent to any remote server.
