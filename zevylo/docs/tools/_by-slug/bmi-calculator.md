## FAQ

**What are the standard WHO BMI categories?**

Underweight: BMI under 18.5; Normal weight: 18.5 to 24.9; Overweight: 25.0 to 29.9; Obesity: 30.0 or higher.

**What is the healthy weight range for my height?**

A healthy weight corresponds to a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9. Zevylo calculates your exact minimum and maximum healthy weight in both kg and lbs.
