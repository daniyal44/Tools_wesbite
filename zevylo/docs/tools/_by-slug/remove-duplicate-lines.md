## FAQ

**How do I remove duplicate lines?**

Paste your list (one item per line) into the input box. Duplicates are removed instantly and the unique result appears on the right, ready to copy.

**Can it ignore case and extra spaces?**

Yes. Enable "Case-insensitive" to treat Apple and apple as the same, and "Trim whitespace" to ignore leading/trailing spaces when comparing.

**Does it keep the original order?**

By default it keeps the first occurrence of each line in original order. You can switch to keep the last occurrence, or enable "Sort A→Z" to alphabetize the output.
