## What it does

Turn typed text into authentic simulated handwritten letters and school notes. Choose from cursive, script, or student print styles, adjust ink colors (ballpoint blue, black gel, red ink), customize lined or legal pad paper, and export as PNG image or print to PDF.

**Can I change the paper to lined notebook paper?**

**How do I download my handwritten notes?**
