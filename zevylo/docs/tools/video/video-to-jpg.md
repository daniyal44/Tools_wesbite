## FAQ

**How do I convert a video into JPG photos?**

Simply upload your video (MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV), select your desired extraction interval or total frame count, adjust your JPG quality slider, and click "Extract JPG Frames". You can also play the video and click "Capture Snapshot Frame" to save any exact moment.

**Can I download all extracted JPG frames as a ZIP archive?**

Yes! Click "Download as ZIP" to immediately package all selected JPG frames into a single zip file directly in your browser without any server upload lag.

**Does this tool preserve video resolution?**

Yes, by default frames are extracted at 100% native video resolution (up to 4K Ultra HD). You can also choose to scale down to 75%, 50%, or 25% if smaller file sizes are desired.
