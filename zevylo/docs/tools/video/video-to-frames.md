## FAQ

**Are my videos uploaded to your servers?**

No. All frame extraction is performed natively in your browser using HTML5 Canvas and video hardware acceleration. Your video files never leave your device.

**What video file formats are supported?**

Standard browser-supported video formats including MP4 (H.264), WebM (VP8/VP9), MOV, and MKV files are fully supported.

**Can I download all extracted frames at once?**

Yes! Zevylo includes an integrated client-side ZIP builder that bundles all your extracted frames into a single ZIP archive (.zip) for instant download with zero server delay.
