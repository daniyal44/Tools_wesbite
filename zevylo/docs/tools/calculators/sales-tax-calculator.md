## FAQ

**How do you calculate sales tax added to a price?**

To add sales tax: Multiply Net Price by (Tax Rate / 100) to find the tax amount. Add that amount to the net price for the Gross Total.

**How do you reverse calculate VAT from a gross total?**

To extract VAT from a gross total: Net Price = Gross Total / (1 + Tax Rate / 100). The tax amount is Gross Total minus Net Price.
