## FAQ

**Does this calculator connect to my crypto wallet or exchange?**

No. All calculations are 100% mathematical and execute client-side in your browser. No wallet connections, logins, or API keys are required.

**How are exchange fees factored in?**

Buy fees are subtracted from your initial investment before purchasing coins, and sell fees are deducted from gross proceeds, providing your true net profit and accurate breakeven price.
