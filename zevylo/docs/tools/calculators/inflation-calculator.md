## FAQ

**What is purchasing power loss?**

Purchasing power loss is the decrease in the amount of goods or services that money can buy over time due to rising prices caused by inflation.

**How is cumulative inflation calculated?**

Cumulative inflation uses the compound formula (1 + r)^n - 1, which compounds annual inflation year-over-year rather than using simple addition.
