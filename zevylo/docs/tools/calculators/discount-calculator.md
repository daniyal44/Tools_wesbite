## FAQ

**How do you calculate a percentage discount?**

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount % / 100). The final price is Original Price minus Discount Amount.

**How do stacked discounts work (e.g. 20% off plus extra 10% coupon)?**

Stacked discounts apply consecutively: The second discount is calculated on the already discounted price, not the original retail price.
