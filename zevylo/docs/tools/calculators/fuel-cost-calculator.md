## FAQ

**How do you calculate fuel cost for a road trip?**

Fuel Cost = (Distance / Fuel Economy MPG) × Fuel Price Per Gallon. For metric units: Fuel Cost = (Distance in km / 100) × (L/100km) × Price per Liter.

**Can I calculate round trips and passenger splits?**

Yes! Toggle round trip on or off, and adjust the passenger slider to instantly see how much each traveler owes.
