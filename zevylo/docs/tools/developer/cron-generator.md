## FAQ

**What is the format of a standard 5-part cron expression?**

Standard cron expressions consist of five fields separated by spaces: Minute (0-59), Hour (0-23), Day of Month (1-31), Month (1-12), and Day of Week (0-6, where 0 is Sunday).

**Can I choose from popular schedule presets?**

Yes! Zevylo includes quick presets for "Every minute", "Every 15 minutes", "Daily at midnight", "Weekdays at 9 AM", and more.
