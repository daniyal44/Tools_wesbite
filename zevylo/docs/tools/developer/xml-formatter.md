## What it does

Format messy or minified XML into clean, indented markup, or compress it down to a single line. Choose 2-space, 4-space, or tab indentation. ## FAQ

**Does it validate my XML?**

Yes. It runs your XML through the browser’s native parser and flags any well-formedness error (mismatched or unclosed tags, invalid characters) with the parser message.

**Can it minify as well as beautify?**

Yes. Use Minify to strip whitespace between tags into a single compact line, or Beautify to re-indent with your chosen spacing.

**Is my XML sent to a server?**

No. Formatting and validation happen entirely in your browser; your data never leaves your device.
